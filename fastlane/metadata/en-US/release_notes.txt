What's new in this version:

NEW COLOURS
The app has a new palette: a warmer purple and a touch of gold for what matters, like how much you've spent when you're on track with your budget.

FIXES
• Restoring a backup brings your expenses back again.
• Editing an expense no longer unlinks it from its piggy bank or its recurring expense, and an unusual payment method is kept.
• Opening Recurring no longer shows charges from months before the rule was created.
• The start-of-month setup in Goals can be finished if you have no income this month.
• Long-pressing a category shows its details for two-word category names too.
• With poor signal, loading your expenses no longer waits forever.
• Siri asks you to unlock your iPhone before telling you how much you've spent.
• Typing in forms with the keyboard open works better.

If something freezes, write to us from Settings → "Algo no funciona" right after reopening the app: the email will include what we need to fix it.
