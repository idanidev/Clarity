What's new in this version:

YOUR FILTERS STAY PUT
The filter you starred as your default was lost when you closed the app, and you were back to "This month". Not any more: it's now saved on the phone itself, so it survives even with no signal at launch.

GETTING STARTED TAKES LESS
Onboarding no longer asks what you earn before letting you use the app. If you want a budget, you set it up from Goals, where it actually does something. And when you finish you no longer land on an empty screen: the mic is right there to log your first expense out loud.

YOU CAN WRITE TO ME NOW
Settings has two new buttons, "Something's broken" and "I have an idea". They write straight to the person who makes the app, which is me. If something bothers you, tell me.

FIXES
• The app no longer hung when opened with no connection.
• Saving an expense offline no longer left the button stuck.
• Inactivity reminders were deleting themselves on launch.
• The onboarding "Skip" button led straight to the screen you were skipping.
