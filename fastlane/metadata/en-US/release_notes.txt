What's new in this version:

• Piggy banks: the "Sacar" (withdraw) and "Alimentar" (add) buttons now display properly when the piggy bank has money. The text used to get cut off.

Since 2.4.2, money you set aside in a piggy bank doesn't count as spending or toward your limits, and you can withdraw it whenever you want.

If something isn't working, write to us from Settings → "Algo no funciona".
