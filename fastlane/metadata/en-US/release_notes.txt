What's new in this version:

• Piggy banks: money you set aside no longer counts as spending. It's no longer free to spend, but it doesn't add to what you've spent or push you over your category limits.
• New "Sacar" (withdraw) on every piggy bank: the money becomes free again whenever you need it.
• Contributions are listed separately, under "Apartado en huchas", and you can edit or delete them.
• When you create a recurring expense's charge by hand, it shows up right away in the list and in its history.

If something isn't working, write to us from Settings → "Algo no funciona".
