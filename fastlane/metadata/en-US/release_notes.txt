And what's new in 2.2:

A HOME BUILT AROUND YOU
The first screen is redesigned top to bottom: your month in big numbers, glass cards and a background that breathes. And it doesn't show everyone the same thing: it looks at your last six months and tells you whether Restaurants is drifting from your usual, how much you'll save if the month keeps going like this, where this month lands among the previous ones, which places you keep going back to, or how much the small stuff adds up to. Every slot shows what matters most to you today. The forecast counts the charges still to come, not just a flat average.

CHARTS YOU CAN TOUCH
Drag your finger across the ring, the days or the months to see the exact figure for each one.

ONE STYLE EVERYWHERE
Goals, recurring expenses, settings, forms, the widget and the icon, which now comes in dark and tinted versions. Recurring expenses get a new screen: how much per month, what's coming up and what's paid in instalments.

DETAILS
• Screens grow out of whatever you tap.
• Confetti when you fill a piggy bank or close a month within budget.
• Bars ripple when you're getting close to a limit.

FIXES
• The app froze for a moment on launch while the month loaded.
• Filters weren't applied to the cards or the charts.
• Changing month from Charts could crash the app.
• The last instalment of a financed purchase was never charged.
