What's new in this version:

YOU DECIDE WHAT THE HOME SHOWS
Home cards can now be hidden and reordered: "Personalizar tarjetas" below them, or long-press a card to remove it. Whatever you put on top shows first.

DATA THAT ACTUALLY TELLS YOU SOMETHING
"Out of the ordinary" now only shows up when you're spending well above your usual in a category you use regularly. Spending less than usual — the month after the garage bill — is no longer news.

GOALS: SAVE X A MONTH
A new kind of goal: tell it how much you want left at the end of the month and it tracks itself from your income and expenses, nothing to top up. It starts from zero every month.

EDIT GOALS WITH ONE TAP
Tap a shield or a piggy bank to edit it. And editing a piggy bank no longer loses its contributions.

FIXES
• A goal you had just created couldn't be edited or deleted until you reopened the app.
